  2. Discuss different types of thermal storage devices.

Model Question Paper

MET445 Renewable Energy Engineering · Maximum 100 Marks · Duration 3 hours

Part A — answer all questions (10 × 3 = 30 marks)

  1. Discuss in brief the advantages of renewable energy.
  2. Explain the following terms related to solar geometry: (i) hour angle (ii) zenith angle (iii) surface azimuth angle.
  3. List different types of solar collectors.
  4. Discuss about solar pond.
  5. List the different methods used to estimate wind speed at a location.
  6. What are the advantages of wind energy conversion systems?
  7. List the geothermal resources.
  8. Discuss advantages and disadvantages of a tidal power plant.
  9. Name the different processes used for hydrogen production.
  10. List the need for economic analysis of a renewable energy system.

Part B — answer one full question from each module

Module 1

  1. Elucidate the necessity of energy storage in the context of renewable sources of energy. (14)
  2. (a) Calculate the number of daylight hours in Srinagar for 22nd June. Latitude of Srinagar: 34°05′N. (4)
    (b) Compare the construction and working of pyranometer and pyrheliometer. (10)

Module 2

  1. (a) How are solar thermal power plants classified? List the methods for converting solar energy into electric power. (10)
    (b) Briefly explain the applications of a solar PV system. (4)
  2. (a) Draw and explain the operation of flat plate collectors. (10)
    (b) Explain the thermal methods of energy storage. (4)

Module 3

  1. With a neat diagram, explain the construction of a propeller-type wind power system. (14)
  2. (a) Derive the expression for power in the wind turbine. (7)
    (b) Explain control mechanism in wind turbines. (7)

Module 4

  1. State the principle of Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC). Explain the working of a closed cycle OTEC system. (14)
  2. Explain the binary cycle geothermal system. (14)

Module 5

  1. Explain the construction and working of a KVIC (floating type) biogas plant. (14)
  2. (a) Define (1) payback time (2) return on investment (3) life cycle cost. (6)
    (b) A solar PV system consisting of two lamps, a battery and other associated components costs Rs. 55,000. The cost of conventional energy saved due to its installation is Rs. 4,000 in the first year and inflates at 5% per year. Assume a discounting rate of 9%. Calculate the payback period of the system with and without discounting. (8)

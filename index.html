Renewable Energy Engineering

Category: Open Elective  |  Credits: 3  |  Prerequisite: Nil

The course is intended to give knowledge of various renewable energy sources, systems and applications and the need in the present context. Students will be able to compare different renewable energy techniques and choose the most appropriate based on local conditions, equipping them to work on projects and take up research in connected areas.

Course Outcomes

CODescription
CO1Explain renewable energy sources and evaluate the implication of renewable energy. Predict solar radiation at a location.
CO2Explain solar energy collectors, storages, solar cell characteristics and applications.
CO3Explain the different types of wind power machines and control strategies of wind turbines.
CO4Explain ocean energy and conversion devices, and different geothermal sources.
CO5Explain biomass energy conversion devices. Calculate the net present value and payback period.
